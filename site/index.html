What we do

Every job starts with the prep: cleaning, patching and sealing, so the paint lasts in South Florida sun and rain.

  • Exterior painting Full house exteriors, trim, doors and walls.
  • Interior painting Rooms, ceilings, trim and doors.
  • Fence painting Metal, wood and concrete fences and gates.
  • Drywall repair Holes, cracks and water damage, patched and painted.
  • Pressure cleaning Walls, roofs, driveways, pavers and pool decks.
  • Stucco repair Cracks, chips and damaged stucco, patched to match.
  • Waterproofing Sealers and coatings for walls and roofs.
