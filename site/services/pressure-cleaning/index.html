Common questions

Can pressure cleaning damage my roof or pavers?

Too much pressure can. We adjust the pressure and nozzle for each surface, especially roof tiles and pavers.

Do I need pressure cleaning before painting?

Yes. Clean walls let the paint stick properly. It's part of every exterior painting job we do.
