Painting and repair services in Hialeah

Seven services, one standard: careful prep, a protected home and a clean finish. Choose a service to see what's included.

  • Exterior painting Hialeah sun, rain and humidity are hard on paint. We prep the outside of your house properly so the new color holds up: clean, repair, prime, then paint.
  • Interior painting One room or the whole house. We protect your floors and furniture, fix the small dents and cracks, and leave clean, straight lines.
  • Fence painting Metal fences rust, wood fences fade and concrete walls get stained. We clean and prep your fence or gate first, then paint it to match your house.
  • Drywall repair From a doorknob hole to a ceiling stain after a leak, we cut out the damage, patch it, match the texture and paint it so you can't tell where it was.
  • Pressure cleaning Mildew and dirt build up fast in South Florida. We pressure clean walls, tile roofs, driveways, pavers and pool decks, on their own or before painting.
  • Stucco repair Cracked stucco lets water into your walls. We open and fill cracks, patch damaged sections, match the texture and paint so the repair disappears.
  • Waterproofing Heavy summer rain finds every crack. We seal exterior walls and roof surfaces with coatings made to keep water out, usually as part of an exterior paint job.
