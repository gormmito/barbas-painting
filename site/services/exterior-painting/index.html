Common questions

How long does it take to paint the outside of a house?

It depends on the size of the house, how much repair it needs and the weather. Most single-family homes take a few days. We'll give you a timeline with your estimate.

Do you pressure clean before painting?

Yes. Paint doesn't stick well to dirt, mildew or chalky old paint, so we clean the walls first.

Can you help me pick a color?

Yes. We can bring color samples and talk through options that work for your house and your HOA, if you have one.
