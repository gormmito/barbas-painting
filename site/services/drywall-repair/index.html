Common questions

Can you match my wall texture?

Yes. We match common textures like orange peel and knockdown so the patch blends in.

Do you paint after the repair?

Yes. We prime and paint the patch. If the old paint has faded, painting the whole wall gives the best match.

Should I fix the leak first?

Yes. Have the leak repaired before we replace wet drywall, or the damage will come back.
